SATURDAY IN ATHENS

Saturday here isn't an event so much as a weather system. It shows up, it takes the whole town, and it does not care what you had planned. If you've got a ticket, this page is about getting in without losing your mind. If you don't, it's about finding a screen and a seat.

WHERE TO WATCH (IF YOU'RE NOT IN SANFORD)

Paloma Park

235 W Washington St

The default answer, and for once the default is right. The courtyard has the biggest outdoor screen in town plus a wall of TVs inside, so there's no bad angle and no fighting for one. Get there well before kickoff on a big game or you'll be standing. Brunch and a pitcher beforehand is its own tradition at this point.

El Paso

255 W Washington St

Right next door to Paloma, which is the whole appeal — if one is packed you walk twenty feet and try the other. Screens around the indoor bar, always on the game, and a calmer room than what's happening outside.

Creature Comforts

271 W Hancock Ave

They put a big screen up under the tent in the yard. It's the move if you want to actually hear the people you came with, and you're already standing in the best post-game spot in Athens when it ends.

Bar South

104 E Washington St

TVs at the front and back bar, pool tables, darts. Less of a scene than the Washington Street places, which on a noon kickoff is a feature.

WHERE TO PARK (IF YOU CAN FIND IT)

Downtown Decks

College Ave & Washington St

First come, first served, cash-ish, and roughly $50 for a single game — check the current rate before you go, it moves every season. Decks open early and fill up early. Closest option that still lets you walk straight into downtown after.

The Classic Center

300 N Thomas St

About a ten-minute walk to Sanford, straight down Thomas. Sold as single-game passes and season packages, and the season ones sell out, so this is a plan-ahead option rather than a roll-up-and-hope one. No tailgating in the deck.

Campus Lots

North, South, East and West Campus

Lots open at 7 a.m. on Saturday and the good ones are gone not long after. Overflow parking runs at the Intramural Fields with fan shuttles from the East Deck on River Road starting about three and a half hours before kickoff — the shuttle is genuinely the least painful way in if you don't mind the ride.

Someone's Front Yard

Basically all of Five Points and Baxter

Churches, businesses, and people who live near campus all rent spots on Saturdays. Prices are whatever the owner feels like, cash is king, and the rules on grills and tents are theirs — ask before you unload a cooler.

The Actual Best Option

Wherever your car already is

If you live close enough to walk, walk. If you're already parked in a deck on Friday, do not move the car. Once the visitors fill everything up you will not get that spot back until Sunday.

TIPS

Get There Two Hours Early for the Student Section

It's general admission down there, so a good seat is purely a function of when you showed up. Two hours before kickoff is the honest number for anything that matters. For a big game, more.

Bring A Fan

September in Georgia does not know that football season started. A small battery fan is the single highest-value thing you can bring, and you will be the most popular person in your row. Sunscreen the same morning, not in the stadium.

Know the Bag Rule Before You Leave

Clear bags only, under 12 by 12 inches, or a small clutch. There's no bag check outside, so anything that doesn't make it through the gate is going back to your car or into a bush. Sort this out at home.

Eat Before You Go

Tate's food options on a Saturday are Chick-fil-A and a long line for Chick-fil-A. If that's not your thing, handle food before you're on campus — everything downtown is also slammed by eleven.

Pick a Meeting Spot

Ninety thousand people on the same towers means your texts will send around Tuesday. Agree on a real physical landmark and a time before you split up, because "just text me" is not a plan inside Sanford.

Don't Book Your Saturday Until the Time Is Set

Kickoff times get announced late — sometimes under two weeks out. A noon game and a 7:30 game are completely different days, so hold your plans loosely until it's official.

The Walk Home Is the Part Nobody Warns You About

Rideshare surges hard and every street around campus is closed or crawling, so leaving is slower than arriving. Wear shoes you can do two miles in, and if you were going downtown anyway, just go — you'll beat the traffic you were trying to catch a ride through.
