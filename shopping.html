Everything but the mall

Boutiques stocked for a night out, vintage racks worth digging through, and a few stores devoted entirely to one shade of red. Almost all of it walkable from downtown.

Back Home

Pitaya

282 E Clayton St

Trend-driven and cheap, with sales constantly. Sizing tops out at large in most styles. Open till 9 most nights.

Shabby Shed Boutique

325 N Hull St

Small, warm, and very much the owner's own — closer to walking into someone's living room than a store. Worth a stop if the big downtown boutiques start to blur together. Open 11 to 7, closed Sundays and Mondays.

Cheeky Peach

160 W Clayton St

The one people name first. Staff will pull a whole fitting room for you if you tell them what the occasion is, and they post new arrivals to Instagram so you can shop before you show up. Open 10 to 6, noon to 5 Sundays.

Dress Up Athens

147 College Ave

Clothes, shoes, and jewelry right in the middle of College Ave. Open till 8 on weekends, which makes it the pre-dinner stop. Returns are store credit only, so try things on.

Heery's Clothes Closet

195 College Ave

A little more elevated than the rest of the block. Good for a wedding, a formal, or anything you need to look put together for. Closed Sundays.

The Swank Company

153 E Clayton St

Apparel, accessories, and a few home things. Sizing runs further than most boutiques downtown, and prices are reasonable for the category.

Epiphany

294 W Washington St

Clothes, jewelry, and gifts, with a share of every sale going to charity. They have their own parking lot, which downtown almost never does. Closed Sundays.

Dynamite Clothing

143 N Jackson St

Downtown vintage that's been here for decades. Levi's, 80s tees, belts, sunglasses. Clean and organized, which isn't a given in vintage. Noon to 6, every day.

Mother Lode

1005 Baxter St

Booth-style vintage with furniture, clothes, and local art. Stock turns over constantly, so it's different every visit. Not thrift pricing, but the browsing is the point. Open 11 to 7 daily.

Agora Vintage

279 E Broad St

Curated designer and vintage, with layaway if you find something you can't quite justify. Hours are odd — split shifts on weekdays and closed weekends, so check first.

Uptown Cheapskate

105 Hickory St

Resale where you can sell your own things too. Clean, organized, plenty of fitting rooms, pet-friendly. Open till 8 most days.

The Red Zone

186 E Clayton St

Licensed Bulldogs everything, from two-dollar souvenirs up. There's a wall of bobbleheads. Open 10 to 6.

The Clubhouse

157 College Ave

Two floors of UGA gear, including pet items and home goods upstairs. Good sale racks, and it's a block from The Red Zone if you want to compare.

Fan Outfitters

198 W Broad St

Hats, jerseys, and monogramming, with the bulldog statue out front. Open till 8, later than the other gameday stores.
