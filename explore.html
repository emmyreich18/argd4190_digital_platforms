The stuff between meals

Gardens, galleries, live music, and a downtown that doesn't quit until 2 a.m. Here's what fills the hours and makes the town feel like more than a campus.

Back Home

Catch a show at the Georgia Theatre

215 N Lumpkin St

The landmark venue downtown, and small enough that every seat feels close to the stage. National acts and local ones both come through. There's a rooftop bar if you want a break from the crowd. Shows Wednesday through Saturday.

Walk the Botanical Garden

2450 S Milledge Ave

Free to get in, free to park, and open 7 a.m. to 7 p.m. every day. Spring is the obvious time to go, but in November they string the whole place with lights for Winter WonderLights. Bring bug spray in summer.

Wander North Campus

Herty Dr, University of Georgia

The oldest part of campus, and the prettiest walk in town. The Chapel, the Arch, and the quad are all within a few minutes of each other. Free, and best in the late afternoon when the light comes through the trees.

Georgia Museum of Art

90 Carlton St

Free admission, free parking in the garage underneath, and a collection that spans American, European, and Asian work. There's a sculpture garden when the weather cooperates. Open till 9 on Thursdays, closed Mondays.

Drinks and a game at Paloma Park

235 W Washington St

The sports bar downtown, with a jumbotron outside and enough patio seating to make a game feel like an event. Tex-Mex, good margaritas, and a Saturday happy hour that's hard to beat. Opens at 5, 3 on weekends, closed Mondays.

Bike or walk the Firefly Trail

Firefly Trail, east of downtown

A paved rail-trail running out of Athens with sculptures along the way. Good for a run, a bike, or just getting out of the neighborhood for an hour. Free and always open.

Saturday at the Farmers Market

705 Sunset Dr, Bishop Park

Produce, meat, eggs, bread, plus jewelry, candles, and food stalls. Live music most weeks. It gets crowded early and prices run higher than the grocery store, but the morning is the point. Saturdays only, 8 to noon.

Go to a game

University of Georgia athletics

Football is the obvious one, but tennis, basketball, and gymnastics are all worth going to and far easier to get into. Student tickets are cheap or free depending on the sport.

Late night downtown

Clayton, Broad, and Washington

Everything is within a few blocks, so the whole night happens on foot. Bars run till 2 a.m. most nights, and Pauley's is open just as late if you need food on the way home.
