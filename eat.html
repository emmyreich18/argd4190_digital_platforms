Here are the best places to eat in Athens!

Breakfast that's worth the wait, dinner that's worth the reservation, and dessert that's worth the walk. Sorted by when you're hungry, not by price.

Breakfast

The Farm Cart

1074 Baxter St

Order at the counter, grab a seat, wait for the biscuit. Cheese grits, house jam, and a line out the door by mid-morning on weekends. Closed Mondays, and they stop at 2.

Mama's Boy

197 Oak St

The Athens brunch institution. Southern breakfast done properly, biscuits from scratch, and a wait almost every weekend. There's a second location out on Macon Hwy if downtown is packed.

Cafe Racer

2343 W Broad St

Biscuits, donuts, and coffee under one roof, opening at 6 a.m. for anyone with an early start. Closed Mondays, and they reopen for dinner Friday through Sunday.

Dinner

ZZ & Simone's

1540 S Lumpkin St, Ste 4

Italian, and the one to book when someone's visiting. Sit at the bar overlooking Lumpkin if you can. Sunday brunch is worth knowing about too.

Osteria Olio

355 Oneta St, Bldg C2

Pizza, pasta, and a wine list in the Oneta complex. Cozy and a little pricier than most things on this list. Reservations recommended.

Pauley's

134 E Clayton St

Crepes, savory and sweet, right downtown. The Bulldawg is the one everyone orders. Open till 2 a.m. most nights, closed Mondays.

The Local 706

1676 S Lumpkin St

Burgers, sandwiches, and a room full of students watching whatever game is on. Parking is limited, as it is everywhere on Lumpkin.

Sakura Hibachi and Sushi Bar

1225 S Milledge Ave

Hibachi and sushi with a solid lunch special. Free parking, which is rarer than it should be around here.

Shokitini

251 W Clayton St, Apt 117

The nicest sushi downtown, with a dark, moody room and an upstairs worth asking for. Dinner only, from 5 p.m.

Trappeze Pub

269 N Hull St

Gastropub with a serious beer list and Belgian fries that deserve their reputation. Good for a group, open till 11 on weekends.

Emmy Squared Pizza

199 Prince Ave

Detroit-style pizza with a thick, crispy-edged crust that people get genuinely worked up about.The gluten-free crust is better than it has any right to be. Indoor and outdoor seating, plus bottomless mimosas at brunch if you book ahead. Open till 10 on weekends.

Taqueria Tsunami

320 E Clayton St

Asian-Mexican tacos you mix and match, plus Korean cauliflower that people order every time. Second location out by the Oconee Connector.

Marker Seven Coastal Grill

1195 S Milledge Ave

Seafood in a converted house in Five Points. Grouper bites, shrimp and grits, and a patio. Parking is tight.

Dessert

Jeni's Splendid Ice Creams

1710 S Lumpkin St

Inventive flavors, generous samples, and open until midnight every night. Vegan options scooped in-store.

Andy's Frozen Custard

2180 W Broad St

Frozen custard rather than ice cream, which makes it richer. Open-air ordering, open till midnight on weekends.

Bruster's Real Ice Cream

1029 Athens W Pkwy

Walk-up window, generous scoops, and a banana split that actually looks like the picture. Open till 10 daily.
